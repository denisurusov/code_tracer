**User:** I think we need to clean artifacts - the lifecycle of a product (thing thats' being built/tested/adopted) and the data model that supports each step. Lifecycle first?

**Claude:** "[…] "clean artifacts" is really about untangling three axes […]: **Product lifecycle** — what *state* the candidate is in. A state machine. […] **Evidence chain** — what *proof* has accrued, L-1…L9. […] **Data model** — what *records* back each state. […] So the clean factoring is: a `product-lifecycle` artifact, a `data-model` artifact, and the architecture doc demoted to the integrating narrative that points at both."

**Claude:** "[…] The **class (a)/(b) divergence is entirely in the top band.** […] From Resolved inward the machine is class-agnostic — that invariant is what keeps the gate a single tool rather than two. […] The **renewal loop is the real modeling fork.** When a bridge sunsets and gets renewed, is that the *same* candidate transitioning back to Commissioned (mutable status, one long-lived record), or a *new* candidate that `supersedes` the old one (append-only, versioned chain)? […] I'd lean toward the superseding-version model, but it's your call and it's the hinge."

**User:** confirming the class a/b divergence being isolated to the top. don't understand this "When a bridge sunsets and gets renewed"

**Claude:** "[…] A backpatch is never meant to be permanent. The real fix lives upstream — but often in a release the bank can't adopt yet […]. Because a privately-maintained fork is itself a liability, the bridge is issued with a deliberate expiry — a sunset date. […] **Retire** — the app has since moved to the upstream-fixed release […]. **Renew** — […] a *replacement* bridge is issued […] and that new bridge runs the whole lifecycle again. That's the loop arrow. […] two immutable records — osera.1 and osera.2 each with their own frozen evidence, osera.2 carrying a `supersedes` edge back to osera.1 […] it's what lets the bank answer a regulator's 'what exactly were you running in March, and what did it cover?'"
