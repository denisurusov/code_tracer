**Claude:** "[…] The-gap is now one item: **alliance governance**. Who may issue, who sits in the root threshold, concrete `k`/`m`/`n`, verifier on/offboarding, compromise procedure."

**User:** Alliance works in two modes: all members vote for a project and share the benefits/spend/responsibility or a single bank goes it alone and acts as the sole governor

**Claude:** "[…] the two modes are the two trust paths. Mode 1 is `osera-root`, mode 2 is `delegated:<bank>`. […] **A solo bank can govern and fund. It cannot supply its own quorum.** In mode 2 the bank is commissioner, consumer, and sole governor. If it also verifies, k-of-n collapses to 1-of-1 and the maker/checker split is gone. […] a solo-mode artifact faces the identical policy: **k independent verifiers, zero dissent, or no promotion.** Which forces a question that has been sitting open since the original chain doc: **is verification a duty of membership, or a credit against funding contribution?** […] You cannot have mode 2 *and* credit-based verification. That's not a preference, it's arithmetic. […] **The vote is evidence.** […] L-1 should carry a `governance` block […]. **Mode 2 has a free-rider problem you can't engineer away.** […] **duty or credit?** It's the one that decides whether mode 2 is real."

**User:** A duty. The 2nd mode is real; it simply removes the need for consensus and leaves the bank solely accountable for its own actions. The others, should they choose to use the outcome, are acting at their own risk
