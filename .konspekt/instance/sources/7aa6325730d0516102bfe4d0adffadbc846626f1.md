**User:** should we make it clear that the bundle is created in steps 1-3?

**Claude:** "Not as stated — that would be inaccurate, and the inaccuracy is one the design deliberately fixed. Two containers, per `artifact-evidence-bundle` §1: **The delivery manifest** — assembled by the maker at Delivered, holding L-1 and L0–L7. It freezes the hand-off and does not cross the perimeter. **The evidence bundle** — assembled by the resolver ③, bank-side, at the Verified → Resolved transition. That is state **04**, not 01–03. What happens in 01–03 is that the *contents* accrue: the contract, the maker's claims, the quorum's VSAs. Assembly and sealing happen at 04, which is also why resolution can be online while verification stays offline — the index is read before the bundle is sealed."

**User:** well this means each bank has to create its own bundle

**Claude:** "Yes — and that follows from freshness rather than from authority, which is the useful part. Walk the contents. Almost every element is produced outside and identical for every consuming bank: the delivery manifest, L0–L8, the contract, the compiled layout, the policy bundle, the Fulcio chain. […] Only two things are bank-specific: the local repository locator for the artifact, and the *recency* of the trust-root snapshot and the checkpoint set relative to that bank's ingest moment. Trust root ≤ 7 days, checkpoints ≤ 24 hours — measured at the gate, so a bundle sealed once for the alliance goes stale and starts failing the freshness rule for whoever ingests later. So the per-bank re-assembly is real, and the cost is small — assembly is discovery and copy, and OCI blob sharing means the shared elements are referenced rather than duplicated. What it actually costs is a **shared identifier**: with n banks there are n manifest digests over what is substantively one evidence set, so no two banks can cite the same bundle to each other or to a supervisor. There is also a seam in the artifact itself. `artifact-evidence-bundle` §1 says the bundle is assembled by ③ bank-side at Verified → Resolved, and `artifact-deployment-topology` §4 says the bundle is the one thing that crosses the perimeter. Both cannot be literally true. What crosses is the element set; the bundle is what the bank seals around it on arrival. **Recommendation — split the container in two:** […] **Evidence core** — assembled by issuer, once, at Fulfilled; digest shared across all banks, the citable identifier; no freshness, frozen. **Ingest envelope** — assembled by resolver ③, per bank, at Resolved; bank-local digest; trust-root ≤ 7d, checkpoints ≤ 24h, measured at the gate. The core carries contract, L0–L8, layout, policy bundle, Fulcio chain. The envelope carries the core digest, the trust-root snapshot, the checkpoint set, and the local locator. Mutualization lands on the part that is genuinely common, independence stays on the part where the bank must verify against material current at its own decision moment, and the verdict gains a `coreDigest` alongside the existing `bundleDigest` […]. A weaker alternative exists: leave assembly per-bank and use the delivery-manifest log entry as the cross-bank identifier. That already has a Rekor digest and is already shared. It covers L-1 and L0–L7 and stops short of L8, the layout and the policy — which is to say it identifies the maker's submission and not the verified evidence set."
