# Goal: Bank-side admission gate + ledger

The product is the on-prem gate and ledger at the *end* of the OSERA supply
chain — not the supply chain itself. It admits externally-hardened open source
into a regulated bank under a verifiable, automated contract, and tracks
exposure and sunset afterward. Root goal; decomposes into the ingest-class
framing, the verification gate, and the domain map.
